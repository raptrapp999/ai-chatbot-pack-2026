## 🛠️ Troubleshooting

### The app won't open

- Make sure you downloaded the correct file for Windows
- Try right-clicking the file and selecting **"Run as administrator"**
- Restart your computer and try again

### No response from a model

- Check your internet connection
- Try switching to a different model
- Wait a few seconds and try again — sometimes the AI servers are busy

### The app looks blurry or small

- Right-click the app icon and go to **Properties**
- Click the **Compatibility** tab
- Check **"Override high DPI scaling behavior"** and set it to **"Application"**

### I can't find my old chats

- Your history is stored locally on your computer
- If you reinstall the app, your history may be cleared
